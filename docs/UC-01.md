# UC-01 — Meal Request → Verified Grocery Cart

**Status:** PROPOSED — awaiting business/design review. This is not an implementation authorization.

**Canonical design:** [2026-11-04 UC-01 Meal-to-Cart Design](superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-uc01-meal-to-cart-design.md)  
**Data contracts & verifier:** [Data Contract](DATA_CONTRACT.md)  
**Tracking:** [Issue #2](https://github.com/hungtvb/mealmate/issues/2)

## Problem

A household shopper wants a home-cooked meal fitting the diners, grocery budget, preferences, declared allergens and available pantry supplies. Manually selecting a menu, calculating amounts and finding purchasable packs is time-consuming.

## Proposed outcome

From a natural-language request, MealMate returns sourced recipes, aggregated ingredients and a draft cart with verified sellable SKU quantities, price, stock and explicit uncertainty. A shopper can revise the menu/cart; there is **no payment or order placement** in UC-01.

## Business scope

- One meal, one store, VND, household shopping.
- Structured curated recipes; ingredient normalization and pantry deduction.
- Product SKU/pack matching; deterministic budget, stock and evidence checks.
- Clear distinction between dislike, allergy and explicit dietary restrictions.
- Follow-ups retain constraints and recompute the cart.

## Design review questions

The canonical design proposes **recipe-first planning** (still awaiting overall sign-off). **Approved budget decision — Option A:** the budget applies to the total price of **whole sellable packages actually purchased**, including leftovers; never prorate by recipe consumption. **Approved portion decision — Option A:** MealMate proposes role-aware portions for the full shared meal using reviewed recipe serving data; user may edit any dish portion, recalculating the draft cart. The LLM does not invent ingredient amounts. **Approved pantry decision — Option B:** independently verified price/stock/pack cart can be accepted while an unknown *pantry* allergen warning stays visible; neither that action nor an LLM response confirms meal allergy safety. **Approved SKU decision — Option A:** a selected supermarket SKU with unknown assessment for a declared allergen blocks cart acceptance until a qualified evidence record or independently verified substitute is available. **Approved fallback decision — Option A:** if the proposed candidate exceeds the purchased-pack budget or a required SKU lacks stock, automatically try another sourced SKU/cart or curated menu, up to **3 complete evaluated candidates total** including the initial one. Keep all user constraints unchanged, present any verified alternative for explicit approval and do not mutate an already accepted cart.

## Acceptance

The canonical design contains **30 proposed acceptance scenarios** including a pinned positive fixture (5 SKU lines, 6 packages, 116,000 VND, `VERIFIED`) and separate negative scenarios for two allergens, evidence identity, unknown pantry labels, expired snapshots and acceptance-time revision conflicts. Read-only explanations preserve a still-current draft; material edits invalidate verification. Acceptance also rechecks recipe and food-evidence revisions. Cart verification, meal-allergen assessment and user acceptance are separate. Cart acceptance **is not** an allergen-safety claim. Full test oracles and contracts live in the linked source documents.

**Implementation is gated** by review/sign-off of the canonical design, a stepwise implementation plan and a separate development PR.
