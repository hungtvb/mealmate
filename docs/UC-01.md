# UC-01 — Meal Request → Verified Grocery Cart

**Status:** PROPOSED — awaiting business/design review. This is not an implementation authorization.

**Canonical design:** [2026-11-04 UC-01 Meal-to-Cart Design](superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-uc01-meal-to-cart-design.md)  
**Tracking:** [Issue #2](https://github.com/hungtvb/mealmate/issues/2)

## Problem

A household shopper wants a home-cooked meal fitting the diners, grocery budget, preferences, declared allergens and available pantry supplies. Manually selecting a menu, calculating amounts and finding purchasable packs is time-consuming.

## Proposed outcome

From a natural-language request, MealMate returns sourced recipes, aggregated ingredients and a draft cart with verified sellable SKU quantities, price, stock and explicit uncertainty. A shopper can revise the menu/cart; there is **no payment or order placement** in UC-01.

## Business scope

- One meal, one store, VND, household shopping.
- Structured curated recipes; ingredient normalization and pantry deduction.
- Product SKU/pack matching; deterministic budget, stock and evidence checks.
- Clear distinction between dislike, allergy and explicit dietary restrictions.
- Follow-ups retain constraints and recompute the cart.

## Design review questions

The canonical design proposes **recipe-first planning** and a hard budget covering **actual packs purchased**, plus an explicit `REVIEW_REQUIRED` status for unresolved allergy/label evidence. These require review before technical implementation.

## Acceptance

Primary scenarios: correct portion and pack calculation, stock/price freshness, known/unknown allergen evidence, unavailable SKU, infeasible budget, shared pantry ingredients and follow-up edits. Full test oracles and data contracts are defined in the canonical design.

**Implementation is gated** by review/sign-off of the canonical design, a stepwise implementation plan and a separate development PR.
