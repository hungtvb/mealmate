# UC-01 — Meal Request to Verified Grocery Cart

**Status: DRAFT — review required before implementation.** This is the first proposed vertical slice, not a completed capability.

## Actor and trigger
A household shopper asks in natural language for a home-cooked meal. Example: "Dinner for four, budget 300,000 VND, no spicy food, one person allergic to shrimp, already have rice."

## Inputs
- Meal occasion/time and number of diners
- Budget amount and currency (MVP: VND)
- Preferences, dislikes, explicitly declared allergens and dietary constraints
- Pantry items/quantities if known
- Single-store catalog, recipes, pricing and inventory with source metadata

## Expected flow
1. Extract structured constraints; ask only for details needed for safe/correct planning.
2. Select recipes with complete ingredients, amounts and serving yields.
3. Scale portions and combine ingredients across dishes; deduct confirmed pantry quantities.
4. Identify purchasable SKUs and pack counts using trusted catalog/inventory data.
5. Apply deterministic budget, quantity, allergen-evidence and availability checks.
6. If constraints fail, explain the conflict and suggest verified alternatives.
7. Present dishes, quantities, sourced price subtotal, uncertainties and draft cart.
8. Preserve constraints on follow-up turns; require customer confirmation before any order.

## Acceptance scenarios
- **AC01** Four diners, 300,000 VND: proposed cart meets portion and budget constraints, or fails transparently if infeasible.
- **AC02** Non-spicy preference: menu avoids explicitly spicy recipes; unknown seasoning is flagged for clarification.
- **AC03** Shrimp allergy: known shrimp ingredient is rejected; unknown labels/cross-contact evidence are not presented as safe.
- **AC04** Pantry contains rice/oil: purchasable quantity is reduced without negative counts; unknown quantity is clarified.
- **AC05** Missing stock or price: no invented availability or subtotal; alternatives must be reverified.
- **AC06** Pack mismatch (need 600g, sold in 500g packs): cart quantities respect actual sellable units.
- **AC07** User changes budget/dish: recompute menu/cart while retaining other active constraints.
- **AC08** Cart cannot become an order or charge payment without an explicit customer approval action.
- **AC09** Conflicting constraints or no feasible menu: say so clearly rather than fabricate compliance.

## Preliminary business rules
- MVP budget applies to catalog **cart subtotal**; tax, delivery and promotions are excluded until modeled.
- Treat "unknown" as distinct from safe, in-stock and priced.
- Medical conditions do not automatically imply a nutrition prescription. Support explicit user-provided restrictions; otherwise clarify or explain limitations.

## Data questions for next design review
- Recipe serving yields and measurement units
- Ingredient taxonomy, aliases and substitution rules
- SKU packaging, purchasable units, price timestamps, inventory freshness
- Ingredient/allergen label provenance and cross-contact uncertainty
- Basket sourcing when a recipe ingredient has no matching SKU

## Definition of done (future implementation)
Trace one realistic request and one failure case all the way from API through tools/verifiers to a draft cart; record deterministic tests and live LLM E2E results separately.
